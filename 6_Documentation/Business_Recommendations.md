# Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations could help improve business performance.

## 1. Improve Customer Retention

The repeat customer rate is approximately 3.12%, indicating a large opportunity to increase repeat purchases.

**Recommended actions:**
- Introduce customer loyalty programs.
- Send personalized offers to previous customers.
- Create post-purchase engagement campaigns.
- Target one-time customers with relevant product recommendations.

---

## 2. Reduce Late Deliveries

Approximately 8.1% of analyzed orders were delivered later than the estimated delivery date.

**Recommended actions:**
- Identify states and sellers with higher delivery delays.
- Monitor delivery performance regularly.
- Improve logistics and fulfillment planning.
- Investigate recurring causes of late deliveries.

---

## 3. Increase Average Order Value

The average order value is approximately 137.75 and customers purchase approximately 1.14 items per order.

**Recommended actions:**
- Introduce product bundles.
- Recommend complementary products.
- Provide discounts for multiple-item purchases.
- Use cross-selling opportunities during checkout.

---

## 4. Optimize Freight Costs

Freight costs represent a significant operational expense compared with item-price sales.

**Recommended actions:**
- Identify regions with unusually high freight costs.
- Analyze seller-level shipping performance.
- Evaluate fulfillment and shipping strategies.
- Balance shipping cost optimization with delivery speed.

---

## 5. Focus on High-Performing Categories and Regions

Sales are concentrated among certain product categories and customer states.

**Recommended actions:**
- Maintain sufficient inventory for high-performing categories.
- Target high-demand regions with marketing campaigns.
- Use regional demand patterns for inventory planning.
- Investigate opportunities in lower-performing regions.

---

## Expected Business Impact

Implementing these recommendations could help the business:

- Increase repeat purchases.
- Improve customer satisfaction.
- Increase average order value.
- Reduce logistics costs.
- Improve delivery reliability.
- Optimize regional and category-level sales strategies.
