# Business Insights

## 1. Customer Retention Opportunity

The analysis identified 96,096 unique customers, of which only 2,997 were repeat customers.

This represents a repeat customer rate of approximately 3.12%.

### Insight
The low repeat-purchase rate indicates a significant opportunity to improve customer retention.

### Recommendation
Introduce loyalty programs, personalized offers, and post-purchase campaigns to encourage one-time customers to return.

---

## 2. Delivery Performance and Customer Satisfaction

Approximately 91.9% of orders were delivered on time, while about 8.1% were late.

Average review scores were higher for on-time orders than late orders.

### Insight
Late deliveries are associated with noticeably lower customer satisfaction.

### Recommendation
Identify high-delay regions and sellers, improve logistics monitoring, and prioritize delivery reliability.

---

## 3. High-Performing Product Categories

The analysis identified strong sales performance in categories such as sports & leisure, computers & accessories, and furniture & decor.

### Insight
A relatively small group of categories contributes strongly to overall sales.

### Recommendation
Prioritize inventory availability, promotions, and marketing efforts for high-performing categories.

---

## 4. Opportunity to Increase Average Order Value

The average order value was approximately 137.75, while the average number of items per order was approximately 1.14.

### Insight
Customers generally purchase a small number of items per order, creating an opportunity to increase basket size.

### Recommendation
Use product bundles, cross-selling, complementary product recommendations, and multi-product incentives.

---

## 5. Freight Cost Optimization

Total freight cost was approximately 2.25M compared with approximately 13.59M in item-price sales.

### Insight
Shipping costs represent a significant operational consideration.

### Recommendation
Analyze high-freight regions and sellers and identify opportunities to optimize fulfillment and shipping costs without negatively affecting delivery performance.

---

## 6. Geographic Sales Concentration

Sales varied considerably across customer states, with a smaller group of states contributing a larger share of sales.

### Insight
Customer demand is geographically concentrated.

### Recommendation
Use regional sales patterns to optimize inventory allocation, marketing campaigns, and logistics planning.

---

## 7. Payment Preferences

Credit card payments represented the largest share of total payment value, followed by boleto and other payment methods.

### Insight
Credit cards are an important payment channel for customers.

### Recommendation
Maintain a reliable card-payment experience while continuing to support alternative payment methods.

> Note: Payment value is not directly comparable with item-price sales because the payment dataset and item-price metric represent different aspects of transactions.
